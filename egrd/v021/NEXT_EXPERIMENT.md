## Falsifiable decision

The cue's adoption claim fails if it does not increase meaningful script use in
the exploratory pairs. More calls with vacuous/wrong-contract evidence, higher
cost or repair regressions do not establish usefulness. Two tasks cannot prove
superiority or a population adoption rate; report individual successes/failures
and unknowns. Even improved adoption with equal Native Resolved is only a
behavioral signal, not repair efficacy.

S0 remains the ultimate deployment benchmark. A later separately authorized
prospective comparison must preserve exact S0 and disclose differences in
Prompt/interface/generation defaults. It is not a pure mechanism ablation and
local resolved fractions must not predict Public Score. Do not replace S0 or
initiate any official submission from this protocol.
