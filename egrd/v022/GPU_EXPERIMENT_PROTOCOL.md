Prespecified decisions:

1. No increase in meaningful valid invocation: no cue retuning or automatic
   rerun; adoption remains unresolved in this route.
2. More calls but vacuous/wrong-contract evidence: not an effective verification
   improvement; contract/reproduction quality is the next research question.
3. More relevant evidence but no useful edit/native gain: adoption signal only;
   investigate evidence-to-action, not more tools.
4. Relevant evidence plus a matched native improvement without unacceptable
   regressions: exploratory positive signal. A **separate** later active/inert
   comparison must hold the cue fixed to study algorithm contribution.
