# DEADLINE-AWARE FINALIZATION
- Form the smallest issue-supported production-code change early, then use the remaining budget to verify and refine it.
- Use get_status periodically to inspect the actual remaining budget: after initial localization, after the first production edit, and before starting another potentially long operation. Avoid redundant status calls when the budget has just been reported.
- Budget from the remaining time and calls, not only the planned call numbers. Reserve enough for the most necessary focused check, inspection of git diff, and submit_patch.
- As the deadline approaches, stop broad searches, whole-repository reading, and optional investigations. Concentrate on the current production fix and the most relevant evidence.
- Bound verification commands by the remaining time after the finalization reserve. If a long check would consume that reserve, choose the smallest useful focused check instead.
- Prioritize delivery of a valid, relevant production patch. Inspect git diff and call submit_patch while time and calls remain; do not postpone finalization for optional exploration.
