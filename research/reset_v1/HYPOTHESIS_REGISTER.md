H1's *deployment prerequisite* has high priority even though its *benefit evidence* is weak. H3/H4/H5 overlap; they must be separately coded, not counted as independent proven explanations. Semantic labels must include OBSERVED/INFERRED/UNKNOWN and reviewer disagreement. No hypothesis authorizes another GPU run or architecture version.

Primary test remains assigned S0 versus assigned V0.2. Conditioning success-rate comparisons on post-randomization Skill activation would introduce selection bias. Activated-only analyses are descriptive; proving a particular mechanism would require a later independently preregistered ablation after total-system benefit is established.
