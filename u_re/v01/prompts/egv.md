Evidence-gated verification:
After a candidate patch, identify its most consequential unsupported behavior contract. Before a targeted check, establish that the input reaches that behavior, runs the changed workspace implementation rather than an installed copy, and can distinguish repair hypotheses. Close the smallest missing link; check module file paths or source identity when imports are ambiguous. Prefer executable assertions over print-only probes or exit zero. Seek a plausible counterexample involving boundaries, exceptions or necessary old behavior, without requiring many tests on a simple task. If a valid counterexample contradicts the patch, revise the relevant production code and rerun the discriminating assertion plus a relevant regression. A broken probe or incorrect expectation is not production counterevidence. Narrow passing checks do not establish complete correctness.
