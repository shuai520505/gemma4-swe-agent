Exact notebook-file bytes and API-return serialization differ, while normalized JSON/content/payload match; do not claim those distinct hashes equal. Publication checks cover known secrets, signed links, full evaluator overlays, decoded Notebook/payload members and raw-file exclusions; they are bounded checks, not a universal proof against every possible information leak.
